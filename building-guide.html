Building Guide

Discover all the charming structures that grow in your kingdom and learn which terrain summons each one.

🏠 Residential Buildings

Cottage

A cozy small home with a thatched roof, perfect for any village.

Best near: Plains, gentle hills

Manor House

A larger estate home with a stone foundation and elegant design.

Best near: Hills, flat plains areas

Village Hall

The heart of community life, appears in larger settlements.

Best near: Central village areas

⚒️ Production Buildings

Water Mill

A rustic mill powered by flowing water, grinding grain into flour.

Requires: River or stream nearby

Farm

Golden fields of wheat and crops stretching to the horizon.

Best near: Flat plains, near water

Orchard

Fruit trees in neat rows, providing fresh produce year after year.

Best near: Hillsides, mixed terrain

Sawmill

A workshop for processing lumber from nearby forests.

Requires: Forest or trees nearby

Fishery

A dockside building for catching fish from lakes and rivers.

Requires: Lake or wide river

Quarry

Stone extraction site for building materials.

Best near: Mountains, rocky terrain

🏪 Service Buildings

Inn

A welcoming tavern with warm lights and comfortable rooms for travelers.

Best near: Roads, village centers

Bakery

The smell of fresh bread fills the air around this charming shop.

Best near: Residential areas

Church

A peaceful chapel with a bell tower reaching toward the sky.

Best near: Hills, elevated areas

School

Where young minds learn reading, writing, and arithmetic.

Best near: Village centers, homes

Market

Colorful stalls selling goods from across the kingdom.

Best near: Central locations, roads

Bathhouse

A relaxing communal bath with steaming hot water.

Requires: Near water source

🏰 Decorative & Landmark Buildings

Windmill

An iconic spinning windmill overlooking the countryside.

Best near: Hills, open plains

Watchtower

A tall tower offering panoramic views of the kingdom.

Best near: Hills, mountain edges

Fountain

An elegant stone fountain with splashing water.

Best near: Village squares, plazas

Bridge

A charming arched bridge crossing rivers and streams.

Statue

A decorative statue honoring the kingdom's heritage.

Lighthouse

Building Tips

  • ✦

    Cluster similar buildings — Placing multiple farms near each other creates agricultural districts.

  • ✦

    Follow water — Production buildings like mills, fisheries, and bakeries thrive near water sources.

  • ✦

    Elevate important buildings — Churches and watchtowers look magnificent on hills and elevated terrain.

  • ✦

    Create districts — Group residential, production, and service buildings for realistic-feeling towns.
