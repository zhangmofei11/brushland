Welcome to Brushland

Draw your kingdom into existence with magical brushstrokes

A cozy sandbox kingdom builder by Transhuman Design

Windows Mac Linux Steam Deck

Paint. Build. Relax.

In Brushland, you wield a magical brush that brings landscapes to life. Draw mountains, rivers, and valleys — then watch as charming buildings and villages grow automatically from your creation. No combat, no death, just pure creative relaxation in a hand-drawn fairy tale world.

How It Works

🖌️

Draw Terrain

Use your brush to paint mountains, rivers, forests, and meadows. Each stroke shapes the land with organic, natural lines.

🏠

Buildings Grow

Watch as cozy cottages, mills, farms, and villages spring up based on your terrain. Rivers bring mills, hills host watchtowers.

🌸

Create Harmony

Balance your kingdom's needs by drawing complementary terrain. Forests attract wildlife, water enables fisheries, plains grow farms.
